Reuse mode: separate-artifact. NO Privacy Badger code, heuristic data, yellow list or
asset is copied into Bedrock, for the same GPL-3.0 reason as uBlock Origin.

Permitted and actually done instead: Bedrock independently implements a tracker-learning
heuristic described in EFF's public documentation (third-party origin observed storing
identifying state across >= 3 first-party sites becomes blocked / cookie-blocked). Source
files implementing it are marked "inspired by Privacy Badger's published heuristic; not
derived from its source".
