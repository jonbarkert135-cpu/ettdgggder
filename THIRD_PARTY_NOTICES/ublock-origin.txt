Reuse mode: separate-artifact. NO uBlock Origin code, list data or asset is copied,
ported, linked or vendored into Bedrock. GPL-3.0 would relicense the browser binary,
which is incompatible with the Chromium base (see docs/LICENSING.md section 3).

Permitted and actually done instead:
  1. Bedrock supports uBlock Origin as a user-installed WebExtension (mere aggregation).
  2. The ABP/uBO filter syntax is a documented data format; Bedrock parses it via
     adblock-rust (MPL-2.0).
Filter lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBO lists) are separate works under their own
licenses (CC BY-SA 3.0 / GPL-3.0) and are downloaded at runtime, never compiled in.
