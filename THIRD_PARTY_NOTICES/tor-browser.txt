Chromium base. Bedrock takes anti-fingerprinting DESIGN only, from the public Tor Browser
design document: uniformity over randomisation, viewport quantisation (letterboxing),
timer coarsening, locale/timezone/language normalisation, restricted font enumeration.

ships no feature named "Tor" and bundles no Tor network integration.
